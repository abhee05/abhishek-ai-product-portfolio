INDmoney SIP Discovery Engine

First-time SIP investors start with good intent, but at their first loss the app shows the number and not what it means — so they go to Reddit or YouTube, then pause or stop. I used an AI discovery engine over 18,489 public investor posts and user interviews to find that moment, then built Before you pause: an AI explainer that appears when they tap Pause or Stop, shows their own numbers and history, declines advice, and offers every option, including Stop.

01

Product Overview

Two working products answer one question. The discovery engine is an AI research tool: it collects public investor feedback, labels each post against the brief's nine questions, and ranks unmet needs in code. Before you pause is the MVP that came out of the research: a screen between a worried tap on Pause or Stop and the decision itself.

Both follow one rule: the AI reads and writes language, code does every count and calculation, and code checks decide what reaches the user.

Goal & Metric Breakdown

The brief's goal: increase the share of first-time mutual-fund investors who still have an active SIP 12 months after their first instalment. The brief ruled out fund recommendations and fear, FOMO or urgency nudges.

North Star · 12-month active SIP rate

First-time investors who paid at least once in days 336–365 ÷ all first-time investors who started that month × 100. Paused two months and came back: counts. Stopped one SIP but kept another: counts. A failed payment: does not count.

First-year SIPs stop in two ways: the investor loses confidence (A), or a payment fails (B). I broke the North Star into five product outcomes along the journey and matched each to engine evidence:

The opportunity score

Each unmet need is ranked by reach × closeness to stopping × first-year weight × controllability. The first three come from the data; controllability is my judgement of how much INDmoney can act on it, and is adjustable in the app.

04

Quality Gates & Golden Set

Every AI label had to pass code checks before it counted: an exact supporting quote from the post, values only from the codebook, and consistent logic. A failing label got one retry, then was excluded — 3 labels were left out.

Did the AI label like a researcher would?

Before looking at any AI output, I hand-labelled a blind, source-stratified sample of 20 posts and compared field by field.

Product (which platform)80% · κ 0.68
Relevance55% · κ 0.39
Drop trigger55% · κ 0.22
Continuation risk55% · κ 0.24
Unmet need35% · κ −0.02
Journey stage20% · κ 0.14
Investor experience20% · κ −0.02

Overall agreement was 46%. The failure pattern was consistent: the AI was too literal, answering unknown or none where I read implicit context. So it under-counts first-year and at-risk signals rather than inventing them — its counts are floors.

Reviewing output also caught a filter bug that let in off-topic YouTube comments; fixing it cut YouTube candidates from 1,953 to 634.

What this result means

46% is not good enough to treat the engine as measurement. I used it to rank where to look and surface exact quotes, then tested the leading need in interviews and a survey. Reporting the weak number is the point: it decided how much weight the engine's output could carry.

07

Problem Definition

Root cause

At the first loss, the app shows the number but not what it means. Its help is built for technical issues, and it sells. So investors leave the app for an explanation — and, without a trusted one, pause or stop.

  • Segment: first-time SIP investors on INDmoney in their first 12 months.
  • Moment: the first time their SIP shows less than they put in, and they open the app to pause or stop.
  • Outcome to move (O2): % who see a loss and still invest 30 days later.
  • INDmoney today (checked 3 and 7 Nov 2026): the Stop screen says "You'll miss out"; the assistant says "I can't decide that for you", then "Reply Yes to stop it"; there is no skip or pause button.
Not "investors stop when markets fall"

The fall is only the trigger. They started with good intent, but no one they trust explains what they see. That reframe moves the solution from market timing (impossible) to explanation (buildable).

Hypothesis: if first-time investors understand their own loss when they tap Pause or Stop, more will still be investing 30 days later. Assumptions to test: doubt, not cash, drives most first-loss stops; people will read an explanation at that moment; honest history does not feel like selling.

Solution Choice (RICE)

Put the help where the doubt happens: when someone taps Pause or Stop.

Option Reach Impact Confidence Effort RICE Decision
Explainer at Pause or Stop530.826.0Chosen
Skip and pause buttons only510.814.0Part of the chosen screen
Learning videos and FAQs810.522.0No: generic, YouTube exists

General AI chat in the app610.430.8No: exists today, loops and sells

My estimates. Reach 1–10, Impact 1–3 (on O2), Confidence 0–1, Effort 1–5. Impact is 3 because it answers the doubt itself; confidence is 0.8 from 6 interviews and 3 MVP tests; effort is 2 because code does the maths on public fund prices and the AI only writes sentences.

Where AI earns its place

It reads why someone came — a loss, cash, "should I stop?" — and explains their own numbers in plain words. A non-AI version (a fixed chart or FAQ) shows everyone the same thing and cannot answer the follow-up question.

09

The MVP: Before You Pause

Open Live MVP
  1. Tap Pause or Stop, and say why. Asked first, so the explanation fits the reason. Giving a reason is optional.
  2. See where you stand. Your own SIP numbers from real fund prices, and how every past SIP like yours did at the same point — bad cases included.
  3. Ask anything. The AI writes the answer and code checks it before it shows. Fund picks and forecasts are declined.
  4. Pick one of five options. Continue, skip once, pause until a month, lower the amount, or stop. Stop is always one tap away.

How it is built

StepDone byHow
Fund pricesCodeLive NAVs for any Indian mutual fund (AMFI data via mfapi.in); saved copies if the API is down
SIP maths and history base ratesCodeReplayed past dips use no future prices
Routing a questionCodeRule-based: advice, prediction, failed payment, cash, options, XIRR, stop or doubt
Declining advice and forecastsCodeBefore the AI: points to the fund's riskometer, a SEBI-registered adviser and investor.sebi.gov.in
Writing the explanationAIGroq, gpt-oss-120b, drafting only from the computed numbers
Checking every answerCode6 checks; one retry, then a written fallback answer

No real SIP is changed, and the app says it is not investment advice. A fund with too little history gets "not enough data to say".

10

Key Product Decisions

Decision · Intervene at the tap

The product appears when someone taps Pause or Stop, not as a general education feed.

WhyThe top need and the highest stop risk meet at that moment. Help anywhere else competes for attention; help at the tap answers a question the user is asking right now.
Decision · Code computes, AI only writes

Every number comes from code; an AI sentence with a number the code did not produce is blocked.

WhyA wrong return figure in a money product destroys trust instantly. Language is where an LLM adds value; arithmetic is where it adds risk.
Decision · Show the bad cases too

History includes every past SIP of the same length, including ones that stayed below the amount invested.

WhyShowing only recoveries would be a nudge dressed as data — exactly what the brief ruled out.
Decision · Decline before the AI

Advice and forecast questions are routed away in code and never reach the model.

WhyThese are the questions users most want answered and the product must never answer. A rule is more reliable than asking a model to refuse.
Decision · Stop is one tap away

All five options are always visible; Stop is never slowed down, and giving a reason is optional.

WhyThe goal is a better-informed decision, not retention at any cost. Blocking the exit would raise saves briefly and destroy trust.
Decision · Cash options first, with no pressure

Skip once, pause until and lower the amount sit beside Continue and Stop.

WhyResearch showed cash crunches matter more than the engine suggested. A tight month should have a softer exit than stopping.

Every AI answer passes six code checks: numbers match the code, no advice, no forecast, no pressure or urgency, the past-performance warning is present, and length.

Go-to-Market & Success Metrics

Launch in stages, and grow only if more SIPs survive without a harder Stop.

1 Validate More first-year investors; legal review of the wording
→
2 Pilot A small share of first-year investors at Pause or Stop; A/B vs today's flow
→
3 Staged rollout All first-year investors, only if the save rate rises and no guardrail worsens
→
4 Scale Every investor; reuse after failed payments and tight months
TypeMetric
North Star% of first-time investors who paid at least once in days 336–365
LaggingFirst-loss save rate (primary): % who see a loss in year one and still invest 30 days later · 12-month active rate, screen vs no screen
LeadingSave rate: % who open the screen and pick anything but Stop · Pause return: % of pauses restarted by the chosen month · Helped: % who answer "Yes, it helped"
GuardrailsTaps from Stop to stopped, no more than today · % of skips or lower amounts followed by a failed debit · Average SIP amount of those who stay
AI qualityGolden set: 100% right on declines, re-run after every change · Live: % of answers blocked, plus 50 answers read by a person each week
Why the guardrails matter

A save that only delays the stop is not a win. Pause return and amount kept catch it, and the taps-to-stop guardrail keeps the exit as easy as it is today.

Risks & Limitations

The biggest risk: an honest explanation read as a promise, or as a push to stay.

RiskHow it's reducedHow we'd know
Read as a promise of recovery (seen in testing)Next version shows the worst case first; a warning beside every numberWeekly review of 50 answers
Feels like blocking the exitStop always one tap away; reason optionalGuardrail: taps to stop
Seen as advice, by users or a regulatorFund picks declined before the AI; 6 checks; legal reviewGolden set: 100% on declines
Keeps people in a SIP they cannot affordSkip, pause and lower shown first, with no pressureGuardrail: bounce charges
Most stops are about cash, not doubtCash options already there; the pilot records reasonsShare who pick "money is tight"
People skip the explanationLead with their own numbers; keep it shortSave rate no better than today
Price data late or wrongFund house price feed; price date always shownWrong numbers in the review
  • Small sample: 6 interviews and 6 survey answers, all salaried, aged 23–35, and only 2 INDmoney users.
  • Directional engine: 46% agreement with hand labels; public posts over-represent complaints.
  • No company data: INDmoney's own numbers are not public.
  • Standalone prototype: it ignores tax and exit load.
What I am not claiming

No retention uplift, user numbers or adoption are claimed. The MVP has not been piloted, so there is no evidence base for any of them.

The point of this build

Using AI to do discovery at a scale one researcher can't, measuring honestly how far to trust it, checking it against real users, and turning the strongest signal into a grounded product moment with guardrails I can test.
