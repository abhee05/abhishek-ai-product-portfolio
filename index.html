About

A builder who thinks like a product manager

Product + AI, in one seat

I bring product discipline to AI work: define the user, their job to be done, and the evidence that proves a solution works — then actually build and evaluate it.

That combination is rare in GenAI right now. Many prototypes stop at a demo. My approach runs the full loop: discovery, scoping, design, evaluation, iteration and launch.

Enterprise-first mindset

Enterprise AI fails for reasons that have nothing to do with model quality — trust, traceability, guardrails and adoption. I design for those constraints from the first requirement, not as an afterthought.

I write about products, pipelines and evaluation in plain language, because AI products are bought and sold on clarity.
