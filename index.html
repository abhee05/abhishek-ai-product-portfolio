01 Enterprise AI / Regulatory research

Regulatory Intelligence Copilot

Problem
Compliance teams can't find the current, applicable regulatory position fast enough to serve the business — and can't prove where an answer came from.
User
Compliance analyst or manager at an Indian NBFC, measured on correctness and traceability rather than speed alone.
My role
Product owner — problem framing, JTBD, MVP scope, evaluation design and iteration.

Product decisions

  • Scoped to RBI KYC / Customer Due Diligence only — one curated domain beats a broad, shallow one.
  • Grounded retrieval over a fixed regulatory corpus, so every claim carries its source citation.
  • Designed "insufficient evidence" as a first-class good answer — for compliance, a refusal builds more trust than a guess.
  • Made retrieval the primary quality gate, measured on a golden question set before judging generation.

Stack Streamlit · Chroma · BGE embeddings · Groq-hosted open-weight LLM

02 Consumer product / Collaborative decision

FoodMood

Problem
Two people can't agree on what to eat because the conversation collects no honest, comparable signal — and it exposes every preference the moment it's stated.
User
Exactly two participants per private session, choosing together across rounds.
My role
Product owner — problem framing, MVP scope, match logic, two-user workflow and iteration.

Product decisions

  • Preferences captured privately, so the answer reflects what each person actually feels.
  • Matches are derived from reactions, never stored — Perfect, Possible, Backup.
  • Only the overlap is revealed; individual reactions are never shown, so nobody defends a taste.
  • Mutual confirmation closes the round — a shared match is a proposal, not the decision.
